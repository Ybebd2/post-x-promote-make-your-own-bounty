# post-x-promote-make-your-own-bounty

Entry for the make.fun bounty `post-x-promote-make-your-own-bounty-01`.

The entry is one post on X by @whoisbagas_ quoting make.fun's
make-your-own-bounty post, with a photo of its own attached:

https://x.com/whoisbagas_/status/2125790451416588389

`POST.json` names that post. `main.ts` (Node 22) copies `POST.json`
to `/output`; the house reads the post itself and scores it on its
replies plus its reposts plus its quotes.
